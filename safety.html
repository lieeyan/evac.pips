Flood Safety Tips

Simple steps that protect your family.

Before the flood

  • Pack a go-bag: water, food, flashlight, power bank, medicines, copies of IDs.
  • Know your barangay's evacuation center and two routes to it.
  • Move appliances and documents to higher ground when a warning is raised.

During the flood

  • Evacuate early when your LGU orders it; do not wait for water to rise.
  • Never walk or drive through floodwater; 15 cm can knock a person down.
  • Turn off the main power switch and gas before leaving.

After the flood

  • Return only when authorities say it is safe.
  • Avoid floodwater: it can carry leptospirosis and electrical hazards.
  • Boil drinking water and report damaged lines to your barangay.
